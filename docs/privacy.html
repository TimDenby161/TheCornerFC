Privacy, corrections and security

Last updated 30 September 2026

Privacy

The Corner FC is a static website. It has no accounts, sign-up forms, cookies, advertising or analytics, and it doesn't collect or store anything about you on a server of its own.

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Images from other sites. Country flags load from flagcdn.com. Your browser contacts that server, so it receives your IP address, browser details and the site's address (thecornerfc.com, not the page you're on). Its own privacy policy applies to those requests. The site doesn't show club badges, competition logos or player and manager photos: initials stand in for them, so no images load from API-Football.
  • Settings kept in your browser. The paper bank and stake size you enter on Model vs Market, and whether to show players' seasons by year, are saved in your browser's local storage (fc.balance, fc.stakePct, fc.playerYears) so they're still there next time. They never leave your device. Clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • FPL predictions and My FPL team. These are for the site owner only. Entering the passphrase sends it to the site's Supabase database (supabase.co) to check it, so Supabase receives your IP address and browser details; the owner's browser then keeps it in local storage (fc.fplKey). Visitors who don't enter one connect to nothing. Nothing about visitors is stored. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • EFL Fantasy. This tab shows expected Fantasy EFL points for Championship, League One and League Two players, worked out from the same API-Football data as the rest of the site. It doesn't connect to the Fantasy EFL game or any other site, and it stores nothing in your browser.

The site shows information about professional footballers and managers (names, clubs, ages, nationalities, match statistics, injuries and ratings and expected fantasy points computed from those). It comes from API-Football and the Fantasy Premier League game's public data. If something about you is wrong or you want it removed, use the corrections route below.

Corrections

If you spot a wrong result, player, club, injury or other data error, please open an issue on GitHub with the page address and what's wrong. Issues are public, so don't include anything private. Ratings, projections and probabilities are model output. They're estimates, not facts, and aren't betting advice. How the models work explains them and how accurate they've been, and the terms of use cover what the site is and isn't.

Security

Please report security problems privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, not in a public issue. The policy is in SECURITY.md, and security.txt has the same contact.
