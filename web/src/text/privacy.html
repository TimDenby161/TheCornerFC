Privacy, corrections and security

Last updated 8 October 2026

Privacy

The Corner FC has no advertising or analytics. You can use all of it without an account, and it stores nothing about you unless you choose to create one. It sets two cookies at most: one that remembers your time zone, and one that keeps you signed in if you sign in. Both are described below.

  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and keeps short-lived records of requests to keep the service running and secure. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Images from other sites. Country flags load from flagcdn.com. Your browser contacts that server, so it receives your IP address, browser details and the site's address (thecornerfc.com, not the page you're on). Its own privacy policy applies to those requests. The site doesn't show club badges, competition logos or player and manager photos: initials stand in for them, so no images load from API-Football.
  • Settings kept in your browser. A Light or Dark theme, if you pick one in the menu, is saved in your browser's local storage (fc.theme) so it's still there next time; it never leaves your device. Your browser's time zone (for example Europe/London) is kept in a cookie (tz, for a year) and sent with each page you load, so that match days and kick-off times are written in your own time; it is used for nothing else and isn't stored on the server. You don't have to accept it: choose UK time at the foot of the menu and the site stops keeping your time zone and shows every time in UK time. The cookie then holds only that choice (off), and My time turns it back on. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both settings.
  • FPL predictions and My FPL team. These are for the site owner only, and are shown once the owner has signed in. Visitors who don't sign in connect to nothing for them. In the owner's browser only, a cookie (owner) remembers to list those pages in the menu.
  • Accounts (optional). If you create an account or sign in, the site's Supabase database (supabase.co) stores your email address, the time you signed up and last signed in, and either a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password or, if you use "Continue with Google", the name, email address and profile picture address that Google passes on. Signing in with a password goes through the site's own server to Supabase; "Continue with Google" takes your browser to Google and Supabase, which then receive your IP address and browser details. The forms for signing in with a password, signing up and resetting a password run a check that you aren't a bot, Cloudflare Turnstile (challenges.cloudflare.com), which loads only when you open one of them. Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details to make that check, and uses them to tell people from bots and to improve the check, not to identify you or show you adverts; the site relies on its legitimate interest in keeping automated sign-ups out (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). See Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum. Google doesn't share your password with the site, and its own privacy policy covers its sign-in pages. This is used only to sign you in and to send the emails that confirm your address or reset your password, which go out through Resend (resend.com). It isn't shared with anyone else or used for marketing. While you're signed in, the sign-in session is kept in a cookie (sb-…-auth-token) that is sent only to this site, only over a secure connection, and can't be read by the page's scripts; it is set only when you sign in, is needed to keep you signed in, and signing out removes it. Resend handles those emails in the United States, under an agreement that includes the UK's approved contract terms for sending personal data abroad. The site holds this information because you asked for an account (the legal basis is the contract with you, UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). An account is kept until you delete it; if you don't confirm your email address within 7 days, the sign-up is deleted. To delete your account and everything above, open Account and choose Delete account. For a copy of what's held about you, or any other question about your account, email timdenby161@gmail.com. See the Supabase Privacy Policy and the Resend Privacy Policy.
  • EFL Fantasy. This tab is shown to the site's owner only, after signing in. It doesn't connect to the Fantasy EFL game or any other site, and it stores nothing in your browser.

People the site covers

The site publishes information about professional footballers and managers. If you are one of them, this section is your notice: writing to each of several thousand people isn't practical, so it is given here instead.

  • What. Your name, club, position, age, nationality and match statistics, and the ratings, predictions and expected fantasy points the site works out from them. Where a club lists you as out, doubtful or suspended for a match, the site shows that too. It doesn't show the injury or illness itself, your date of birth, or a photo.
  • Where it comes from. API-Football, a commercial football data service. Fantasy Premier League's public data is also collected, and is shown only to the site owner.
  • Why, and the legal basis. To publish football statistics, ratings and predictions. The basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): reporting and analysing professional sport that is played in public. Whether you are available for a match is health information, used because you or your club made it public (Article 9(2)(e)). Ratings and predictions are a model's estimates of sporting performance, not facts about you, and nothing is decided about you from them.
  • How long. Your profile and statistics stay while you play in a league the site covers, and for the past seasons its pages show. Availability for a match drops off the site within three weeks of that match. The reason behind it is never published; it is held privately for the models for the current season and the one before, then deleted. Injury notes from Fantasy Premier League are held privately and reviewed for deletion each summer. Older copies of the site's data files stay in the public change history of the site's code until that history is rewritten, which can be done for your files if you ask.
  • Your rights. You can ask for a copy of what's held about you, for it to be corrected, restricted or removed, and you can object to it being used. Requests are free and are answered within a month. If you are removed, the site stops collecting your information as well as deleting what it holds. Requests about players under 18, from them or a parent or guardian, are acted on without question.

The site is run by Tim Denby, who decides how this information is used (the data controller): timdenby161@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, email that address first. You'll get an acknowledgement within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Corrections

If you spot a wrong result, player, club, injury or other data error, please open an issue on GitHub with the page address and what's wrong. Issues are public, so don't include anything private. If the information is about you, or you'd rather not ask in public (a correction, a removal, or anything about your health), email timdenby161@gmail.com instead. Ratings, projections and probabilities are model output. They're estimates, not facts, and aren't betting advice. How the models work explains them and how accurate they've been, and the terms of use cover what the site is and isn't.

Security

Please report security problems privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, not in a public issue. The policy is in SECURITY.md, and security.txt has the same contact.
