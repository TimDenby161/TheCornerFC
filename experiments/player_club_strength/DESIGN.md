# Player / club strength experiment protocol

Production functions are imported for read-only computation; production files, constants and database rows are never modified. No API-Football calls. Freeze the database extract in an enforced read-only repeatable-read transaction. Record hashes and coverage.

This is a retrospective chronological reconstruction, not a prospective test. Two daily immutable player captures are insufficient for transfer validation. Current production season ratings use career/age smoothing and are not an admissible historical predictor: historical revisions, all-period normalization and potentially later career evidence can leak. Evaluate their current cross-sectional dependence descriptively; evaluate the production rolling match-player formula with chronological normalization separately. Do not call that replay the exact website season rating.

- Norms, league rating offsets and percentile reference: fixtures before 2022-01-01 only.
- Downstream training: 2022-01-01 to 2023-06-30; validation: 2023-07-01 to 2024-06-30; test: 2024-07-01 onward.
- Last-20-appearance / 540-day windows updated only after a fixture; all fixtures at the same kickoff are scored before any are ingested.
- Variants: production `final_rank` with historical window club level; the pre-club stat percentile; `final_rank` with club fixed at 1000 (removes explicit club-strength term while retaining position transformations); production-formula rating residual against position-specific training-only quadratic club expectation. Also test stored historical production player ranks only if their provenance supports chronology; otherwise omit them.
- At least 180 historical minutes for eligibility. Candidate population: players appearing in the club's previous five observed matches, including zero-minute outcomes when subsequently absent. No future lineups used as inputs. Performance endpoint: next-match adjusted provider rating conditional on at least 30 observed minutes and a nonmissing rating; explicitly selection-biased, not a pure measure of talent. Minutes: next-match minutes (absence=0 only when both teams have adequate observed lineup coverage).
- Player baseline controls: current/long-term club and opponent strength, home indicator, age, position and previous-five-match minutes share. Compare identical eligible rows for each target. Ridge hyperparameter selected by validation MSE, then refit training+validation. Report held-out MSE/MAE and paired player-cluster bootstrap intervals.
- Match endpoint: 1X2 multinomial logistic model. Club-only baseline includes Current/Baseline home-away differences, mean club strengths and competition indicators. Compare the baseline with home-away predicted-XI rating differences. Predicted XI: one keeper plus ten outfield players selected by previous-five-match minutes with eligible prior scores; require 11 on both sides. No injuries or actual future starting lineup in selection. Validation log loss chooses regularization; refit training+validation. Report paired weekly-block log loss/Brier intervals, calibration and accuracy on identical fixtures.
- Transfer proxies: change in observed domestic club among covered competitions, not verified transfer transactions; omit gaps over 365 days. Strong-to-weak / weak-to-strong require at least 50 LT points. Track first-new-club and fifth-new-club scores versus last-old-club score; compare score continuity and association of score change with club change. Continuity alone is not proof of quality. Evaluate predictive targets on the first five new-club appearances separately. Coverage exits and nonplaying transfers remain unobserved.
- Current correlations: Pearson and Spearman by position; within-club SD, interdecile range and between-club variance fraction. No causal interpretation of correlations.
- Uncertainty: 1,000 deterministic paired cluster-bootstrap draws; pointwise, no multiple-testing adjustment. Test-set comparisons descriptive; do not retune the model on them. Practical review thresholds: at least 0.002 match log loss with corroborating Brier, or 1% improvement in player-target MSE, stable across time periods and with interval support. These are review criteria, not automatic deployment.

Existing model constants were designed with historical data, including portions of these dates. Training-only replay prevents normalization/window lookahead but cannot undo historical model-selection leakage or revisions. Opponent/context effects and provider ratings are not club-independent ground truth. Results should motivate prospective collection, not production changes.

## Coverage adjustment and robustness check

The pre-2022 training data has no usable centre-back `team_xga` normalization. Omit this metric from every replay variant; all other available statistical weights are unchanged. This is an experiment-only reconstruction deviation, not a production edit.

Player regressions additionally control for competition. Following the first diagnostic fit, add a stricter historical-club control sensitivity: player-position-specific linear/quadratic window club level for player targets, and predicted-XI mean window club levels and mean squares (home-away difference and combined mean) for match targets. This checks whether the scaled rating merely repackages club history. Treat this as a post-design robustness check, not a preregistered independent confirmation. Also report the twentieth new-club appearance to reduce the mechanical continuity caused by the 20-appearance window. Later-appearance cohorts condition on remaining observable and playing enough matches.
